# Shared expression semantics

Reaction is automatic presentation: the H5 semantic selector chooses a reviewed meaning, and the robot maps it to its own existing face and supported head gestures. Do not run a separate emotion classifier, infer success from a cheerful expression, or use Reaction as authorization for tools.

Explicit `robot.move` and `robot.gesture` own the head until completion/cancellation; automatic cosmetic movement yields. Faces and task badges may continue. Task badges reflect pending work; they do not prove task success or replace conversational emotion.

Do not claim robot Reaction is the same bitmap or the same physical pose as H5. Only the meaning is shared, adapted to two axes. Candidate tests with mocked servos are not real hardware acceptance; audio, image and movement each need their own evidence.
